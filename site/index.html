AIS Gateway

Firmware that puts AIS from an NMEA 0183 receiver or transponder (RS485/RS422) onto the NMEA 2000 bus. For the M5Stack Atom Lite with a CAN Unit and a Tail485. Version ….

This browser can't install firmware. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over https to install.

Connect the Atom Lite to this computer by USB-C, click Install, and choose its serial port. Install before fitting the Tail485, while the USB port can be reached. Erase the device on a first install. To update a gateway you've set up, don't erase: its settings are kept.

After installing, "Logs & Console" shows the serial log, including the line Device password: ….

After installing

  1. The gateway starts its own open WiFi, AIS-Gateway-XXXX. Its LED blips purple twice every 5 seconds.
  2. Join it with your phone. The setup page usually opens by itself; if not, open http://192.168.4.1.
  3. Choose the boat's WiFi network (names are case-sensitive), enter its password, and check the AIS baud rate. Note the device password shown on the page, or set your own: it protects updates, the settings and the gateway's own WiFi.
  4. Save. The gateway restarts and joins the boat's network.

Later: the status page is at http://ais-gateway.local/ on the boat network, with Settings and Firmware update. If the boat network is missing for 5 minutes, the gateway starts its WiFi again, this time protected by the device password.
